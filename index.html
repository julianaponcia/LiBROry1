Frequently Asked Questions

How does book borrowing work?

Navigate to the "Borrow a book" tab, select an available title, and log your borrowing schedule instantly.

Is LiBROry fully safe and free to use?

Yes! The system utilizes local storage to securely track user accounts, active inventories, and history.

How do I submit book reviews?

Head over to the "Review a book" tab to rate titles and share feedback with other readers.
